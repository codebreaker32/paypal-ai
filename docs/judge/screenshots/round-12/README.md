# Round-12 screenshots

Generated by `e2e/screenshots.spec.ts` at 390×844 and 1440×900, with an axe check on every screen (`axe-results.json`).

Some states are **fixtures**, not live payments: the paid, shipped and expired receipts, the dashboard orders and the order detail pages use orders written straight into the e2e database with fake PayPal ids that start with `E2E`. The console conversation is replayed from a recorded run. Real sandbox payments are shown in `../capture/` (the capture gate: guest checkout → Paid → AI brief → USPS tracker) and `../task7/real-*.png`.

The in-between states are **mocked or fixtures** too. The app code is unchanged; the test answers the request in the browser or writes the database:

- `setup-publishing`: **mocked**. The publish stream is answered in the page with the start line and 6 "added" lines (fake `PROD-E2E` ids), then held open.
- `setup-publish-retry`: **mocked**. The publish stream is answered with 2 items failing as PayPal 503s (the app's own seller message) and the shop live with the rest.
- `console-thinking`: **mocked**. The console's stream is answered in the page with its session line, then held open.
- `console-rate-limited`: **mocked**. The recorded first turn, then a 429 `RATE_LIMITED` (the server's message) for the next one.
- `console-error`: **mocked**. The recorded first turn, then the `error` line the server sends when the assistant fails mid-turn.
- `order-brief-writing`: **fixture**. The nut-allergy order, paid just now with no brief yet, on a second "Marigold Bakehouse" fixture shop (so it stays off the demo dashboard).
- `order-tracking-error`: **mocked**. The tracking POST returns 502 `PAYPAL_REJECTED` "PayPal said: Tracking number is invalid for carrier USPS."
- `dashboard-paypal-unreachable`: **fixture**. The demo shop's open orders are stamped as last checked with PayPal 5 minutes ago, as a stalled poller leaves them.
- `receipt-paid-to-seller`: **fixture**. A paid order of a shop whose PayPal email is the made-up `rosa@example.com`, so the receipt shows the masked form. The real seller payout (a US sandbox buyer paying a US sandbox seller by PayPal login, payee checked on PayPal's own record) is the capture gate's seller test in `../../round-6-tests.txt`; its PayPal pages aren't screenshotted because they show the sandbox login.
- `order-payment-held`: **fixture**. An order whose capture PayPal holds (`PENDING`, reason `UNILATERAL`) because the shop's email has a typo (`rosa@exmaple.com`), as the sandbox does for an email with no PayPal account (verified in a spike, not by this test).
- `dashboard-loading`: **mocked**. On a production build the test opens an order and clicks back to Orders; the page's RSC request for the dashboard is held in the browser, so `(dashboard)/loading.tsx` stays on screen. Nothing reaches the server.
- Snap your counter's states: `dashboard-menu-counter` (**mocked**: three proposals answered in the page with crops of the demo shop's own photos), `dashboard-menu-counter-reading` (**mocked**: the request is held, so the sheet stays on *Finding your items…*), `dashboard-menu-counter-none` (**mocked**: an empty answer), `dashboard-menu-counter-error` (**mocked**: the route's own 502 `COUNTER_FAILED` message), and `dashboard-menu-counter-live` (**live**: the real route and a real Claude answer for `public/samples/counter-case.jpg`).

**The design reset (spec §4b), and run 3's Part A on top of it.** Every screen is in the photo-first "Storefront" design (`docs/DESIGN.md`); the demo shop's photos are Pexels photos credited in `docs/ASSETS.md`. New in run 3 (the human's feedback of 6 Nov):
- **Images, never text stand-ins.** No initials or letter tiles anywhere. An item with no photo shows one of 13 Shelfie drawings chosen by its category (Claude picks it while reading the menu; contact sheet `../../evidence/illustrations.png`), on its section's tint; the shop's picture is its logo, else a detail of its cover, else its menu photo, else a drawing. `shop-no-photos` and the setup screens show the drawings; `shop-390`/`shop-1440` show the cover detail as the shop's picture.
- **Colour roles.** Errors use their own vermilion (`setup-failed`, `dashboard-settings-invalid`, `receipt-capture-refused`, `order-tracking-error`), never the pomegranate accent; the AI brief and allergy notes are a calm slate note (`order-paid-with-brief`).
- **Phone shop rows** put the price under the name; section jumps land below the sticky tabs and above the order bar (checked in `e2e/shop.spec.ts`). **The start page's** floating cards no longer clip text.
- **Motion** can't be judged from stills: these screens are taken with reduced motion, so they show every animation's final state. The motion itself is in the screen recordings, `../../recordings/round-9/`.
