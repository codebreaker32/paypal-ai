# A real paper menu for the home page (run 8)

The owner asked for "a real menu written on paper instead of AI gen image" for the hero chain's first card and How it works step 1. The brief: a licensed photo (Pexels or Unsplash licence only) of a handwritten paper menu or price list, black marker or ink on cream or off-white paper, items with prices beside them, legible, warm, no brand names, faces or trademarks. The owner's style reference was used as inspiration only and is not in the repo.

Searched: Pexels "handwritten menu", "handwritten price list", "paper menu", "handwritten menu paper", "handwritten list notebook", "menu clipboard", "handwritten menu bakery", "hand written menu on paper", "menu written by hand", "handwritten coffee menu"; Unsplash "handwritten-menu", "hand-written-menu", "paper-menu", "handwritten-price-list", "menu-handwritten", "handwritten-cafe-menu". About 140 results screened from thumbnails; most were chalkboards, printed menus or blank clipboards. Six real candidates were downloaded (`/tmp/menu-cands/1..6`) and compared side by side in `docs/judge/menu-candidates.jpg`.

**Pick: 1 (Bundo Kim).** It is the closest to the reference's qualities: a casual, real, hand-written list in black ink on cream paper with a price beside every item, in English, with nothing that names the café. It is shot at an angle in a dark room rather than from above, and it has no doodles; the hard window light is evened out with CLAHE and brightness +35% so the ink reads at the hero card's ~230 px. Crops: 3:4 on the right-hand sheet for the hero card (`public/landing/paper-menu.webp`), 4:3 for step 1, the photo alone (fix round: the drawn "We read" card was removed because it was invented UI, not the app's own reading) (`public/landing/step-read.webp`), 16:9 full frame for the video (`video/project/assets/paper-menu.jpg`). Credits in `docs/ASSETS.md`.

**Open for the owner.** This photo is a sheet framed on a wall, shot at an angle in hard light, not paper from above on a dark table with doodles like the reference, and it reads darker than the white cards beside it. A fix-round web search (Pexels and Unsplash, "handwritten menu paper prices", "handwritten menu on paper cafe") found nothing closer; a better match needs either a new licensed find or a photo the owner takes of a real hand-written menu (then rerun the crops with `/tmp/r8fix/assets.mjs`-style sharp steps: 3:4 480×640 and 4:3 960×720 WebP).
