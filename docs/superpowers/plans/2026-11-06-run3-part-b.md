# Run 3 Part B: the demo video — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A finished demo video under 3:00 (aim 2:30–2:50), 1920x1080 at 30 fps, with a local Kokoro-82M voice-over, burned-in captions and an `.srt`, showing Shelfie working end to end on the final Part A UI.

**Architecture:** The demoday pipeline drives it: a `demo.json` manifest with one narration line per scene, voice first so every scene's length is known, then capture paced to the voice, then a Remotion render with captions from word timings. Two changes to that pipeline. The voice comes from Kokoro-82M on the CPU, not fal or ElevenLabs: a Python adapter writes the same `mp3 + .words.json` files that demoday's `gen/tts.mjs` writes. Scenes that demoday can't capture on Linux (the terminal with Claude Code, PayPal's login cut) reuse the project's own clip recorder (`npm run clips`, Xvfb + ffmpeg).

**Tech Stack:** demoday (skills `demoday:create-demo-video`, `demoday:demo-video`, `demoday:demo-script`, `demoday:demo-capture`, `demoday:demo-assembly`) plus the `demo-video` skill (Remotion patterns). Remotion 4 lives in `video/remotion/`. The voice is Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0, `video/.venv`, gitignored), and ffmpeg does the encoding.

**Spec:** the run brief's Part B; `docs/DEMO.md` (facts we may say); `docs/JUDGING.md` (A5, Part C).

## Global Constraints

- Under 3:00 (aim for 2:30–2:50), 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC.
- **Story:** (1) the problem, who it's for and why now, in plain words with real stakes; (2) snap a paper menu, and the shop goes live with photos; (3) Claude Code, given only the shop's URL, finds the agent door and orders; (4) the buyer approves on PayPal with the sandbox wallet login; (5) the seller's dashboard: paid, the AI brief flags the nut allergy, tracking added; (6) why it matters, and close. Show the product working, not slides.
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M on the CPU with a natural English voice. Record the model and its licence in `docs/ASSETS.md`. No music (none is fine).
- **Captions:** burned in, plus `video/out/shelfie-demo.srt`, committed as `video/shelfie-demo.srt`.
- **Nothing private in any frame:** mask owner links, emails, tokens and keys. Sample at least one frame every 2 s and record the check.
- **No third-party brand logos or marks,** except PayPal's own checkout pages, which are part of the flow. No reference sites.
- Every claim spoken comes from `docs/DEMO.md` "Facts we may say" or a verified run.
- **Outputs:** the Remotion source and the `.srt` are committed. `video/out/shelfie-demo.mp4` and `video/out/poster.png` are gitignored. Put the mp4's path and duration in `docs/PROGRESS.md`. Don't upload anywhere.
- One clip recording or one render at a time, never alongside a full e2e run.
