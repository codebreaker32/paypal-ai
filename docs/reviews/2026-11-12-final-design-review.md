# Final design review (run 16, 11–12 Nov 2026)

Review only, no product code changed. The owner decides what gets fixed.

## Verdict

1. **Ready to submit once two quick fixes land** (about 20 minutes each). The counter card's shop name overlaps itself at desktop width, and the home chat offers "Make it pickup instead" on an order that is already pickup.
2. All three lenses agree it looks like a finished product, not a proof of concept. The buyer flow, the seller dashboard, the receipts and the error and empty states all hold up at 390 and 1440, and axe found no serious or critical violations on any of the 102 suite screens.
3. Part C anti-slop: 0 banned patterns on home, shop, cart, consoles, receipts, orders and order detail. The only screens with two are `setup-review` and `review-paypal-email`: the example-photo disclosure is shown three times, and there are boxes inside boxes (F6). "Images, not stand-ins" passes everywhere.
4. The rest is coherence a judge notices while clicking around: the assistant goes by two names (F3), "Your order 0" shows right after the assistant has placed an order (F7), "Repairs" on Browse becomes "Services" on the shop page (F13), and Terms contradicts Help (F12).
5. The 13 Must-fix and Should-fix findings were each re-checked against the screens by a separate verifier. 11 were confirmed (2 Must-fix, 9 Should-fix, 2 of them downgraded to Nice-to-have) and 1 was dropped as a deliberate, documented rule. Nothing here is L effort. The fix list below fits in about 2 hours.

## How this was done

- **Screens:** 165 screenshots in [`final/shots/`](final/shots/README.md), all from production builds (`next build && next start`). The seeded set ran against a throwaway DB seeded with `npm run seed -- --reset` (six live sandbox shops); its script is `final/capture-public.ts`, and the home chat shots are a real Claude turn. The suite set came from `npm run screenshots:prod`: setup, dashboard, order detail, receipts and console states. Every email shown is an invented fixture, and no owner link or token appears on screen.
- **Three independent lenses**, each a separate agent that saw only the screenshots, the running app and the code: **A** design:design-critique (13 findings), **B** impeccable (17), **C** design-taste-frontend-v2-experimental plus JUDGING.md Part C (11).
- **Merge:** the 41 raw findings became 24 unique ones. Most of the important ones were raised by two or three lenses on their own.
- **Verify:** fresh agents checked each Must-fix and Should-fix against the screenshot, the code and the live app.

## What's genuinely strong

- **The AI is content, not decoration.** The order brief flags "Buyer mentions a nut allergy, but almond croissants contain nuts" and checks PayPal's amount against the order. Setup quotes what Claude read and explains each uncertain price. The home chat builds item cards from its real tool results.
- **The palette is disciplined across about 100 screens.** Pomegranate is the one accent, PayPal blue appears only on "Approve $X on PayPal", errors are a separate vermilion, and status is always a dot plus a word, never a wall of chips. Figtree is the only family, with tabular totals.
- **The seller dashboard reads like Square or Toast:** grouped orders with subtotals, one action per paid row, a master-detail order view, and skeletons shaped like the list.
- **Responsive work is real:** container-query tile grids, a shared cart and assistant dock with a switch from 1024 px, true bottom sheets on phones, a tab bar replacing the sidebar, and no horizontal scroll anywhere.
- **The states are thorough and honest:** expired, awaiting, refused and held receipts, PayPal unreachable, publish retry, the console rate-limited, all with plain copy that names the fix.
- **Real images everywhere:** seller photos, labelled example photos and drawn category fallbacks. No initials, emoji or stand-ins.
